How the safety
net works.

      

        
DeskDora asks before it acts. Before anything
          risky — deleting, sending, submitting, installing — it
          stops and shows you the exact step. Nothing happens until you say
          yes, and no answer means no.

        
Money always asks. Payments and purchases can
          never be set to run on their own; a person approves every one,
          every time.

        
Every action is on the record. Each step is
          written to an audit log with a screenshot, so you can replay
          exactly what happened — and the stop control is always on
          screen if you want it halted now.

        
There is no switch that turns the asking off. You can
          loosen or tighten it per kind of task later, in Settings.
